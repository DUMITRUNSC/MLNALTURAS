## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Design an agency-quality complete visual branding concept presentation ONLY for the brand Orfactu, an invoicing software product for freelancers and small businesses in Spain. A bright large landscape editorial brand board on uniformly ivory #F7F6F0 background. Brand strategy is clear everyday organization of quotes, invoices and payment tracking. Main exact wordmark "orfactu" lowercase, beautifully crafted friendly but rigorous sans serif lettering. Original minimal solid dark green O symbol with a subtle folded document corner, simple and vector-friendly. Large dark green logo on bright ivory in top left, extremely legible. Exact headline "Tu facturación, en orden." in dark ink. Three small exact workflow words "Presupuesto", "Factura", "Cobro" linked with clean arrows. Palette forest green #174C43, ink #182522, ivory #F7F6F0, pale lime #D8E9A5 and white. Show labeled color swatches, typography sample "Claro. Sencillo. En orden.", a dark green reversed logo tile, a premium white business card and ivory welcome guide with the orfactu logo. Include ONE large realistic laptop mockup with a clean minimal invoicing product concept UI in Spanish: clear list of invoices, label "Facturas", button "Crear factura", fictional client names "Cliente demo", separated payment status "Pendiente de cobro"; ONE phone mockup showing simple document preview with a button "Descargar PDF". These are illustrative product concepts. Keep screen content minimal and readable, believable simple software. All branded applications coherent with the same wordmark and O symbol. Art direction tactile, calm, trustworthy, generous whitespace, strong typographic hierarchy and a considered asymmetric grid. At bottom small exact text "DIRECCIÓN DE MARCA · CONCEPTO 01". No gradients, no dark overlays, no glow, no vignette, no complex abstract 3D marks, no seals, no certification claims, no legal compliance claims, no prices, no invented performance statistics, no MLN, no construction imagery. Main logos high contrast on bright background. The visual should feel like a refined accessible productivity brand, not luxury perfume.
